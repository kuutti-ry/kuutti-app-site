## Ask a question of your own

This site has no form, and it sets no cookie. Send your question to Kuutti ry where you already are, on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/kuutti_ry/), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kuutti_ry) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/145260967/).

- Look through the questions above first: yours may be there already.
- One question per message, in your own words.
- Never send your personal identity code, your bank's codes, or anything you would not show a stranger.
- A bug or an idea for the app can also go to [Kuutti's issues on GitHub](https://github.com/kuutti-ry/kuutti-app/issues), where everybody can read it.

When many people ask the same thing, the answer is added here.
