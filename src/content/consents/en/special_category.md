DRAFT: Whom you seek, the parties you could vote for and your religion are special categories of personal data. If you say yes, Kuutti stores the answers you give to these questions: whom you seek only to match you, never shown to anyone; the parties and the religion on your card, when you choose to fill them in. None of them ever goes into a log or a research event, and you can withdraw this consent at any time, which removes the answers. Without your yes these questions stay unanswered.
