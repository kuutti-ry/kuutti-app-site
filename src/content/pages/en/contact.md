## A security problem

Please do not report a security problem in public. Write to [security@kuutti.app](mailto:security@kuutti.app), or use the private form at [github.com/kuutti-ry/kuutti-app/security](https://github.com/kuutti-ry/kuutti-app/security/advisories/new), for the app and for this site. You get an answer within seven days.
