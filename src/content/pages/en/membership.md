Kuutti ry has members of two kinds, as its [bylaws](/legal/bylaws/) set out (sections 3, 4 and 8).

## Voting members

A private person who accepts the association's purpose can be a voting member. Voting members have one vote each at the association's meetings once they have been members for twelve months; the founding members voted from the start.

## Supporting members

A private person or an organisation that wants to support the association's purpose and work can be a supporting member. Supporting members may attend the association's meetings and speak there, without a vote.

## How to join

Write to [hello@kuutti.app](mailto:hello@kuutti.app) with your name and the kind of membership you apply for. The board decides on every application and answers you.

The annual meeting sets the joining fee and the yearly fee, separately for each kind of member.

## What membership does not change

Being a member, of either kind, or not being one, makes no difference to anybody's place or benefits in the app. That is in the bylaws (section 3).

A member can leave at any time by telling the board in writing; a voting member can become a supporting member the same way (section 12).
